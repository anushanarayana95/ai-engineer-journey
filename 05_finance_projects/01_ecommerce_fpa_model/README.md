# E-commerce FP&A Financial Model & KPI Dashboard

A synthetic finance portfolio project demonstrating practical FP&A modelling in Excel.

## What this project covers

- Monthly P&L structure
- Revenue and forecast modelling
- COGS and gross profit
- Gross margin and EBITDA
- Marketing, payroll and operating expenses
- AR/AP days and working-capital concepts
- Operating cash flow and ending cash
- Base, downside and upside scenarios
- Management KPI dashboard

## Business question

How do revenue growth, costs and working-capital assumptions affect profitability and cash?

## Tools

- Microsoft Excel
- Financial modelling formulas
- KPI dashboarding

## Interview explanation

"I built a synthetic e-commerce FP&A model in Excel. I started with monthly revenue and built the P&L through COGS, gross profit, marketing, payroll, other operating expenses and EBITDA. I then added AR and AP day assumptions to connect working capital with operating cash flow and ending cash. Finally, I created base, downside and upside scenarios to test how changes in assumptions affect profitability and cash."

## Important

All figures are synthetic and are not company or client financial data.
